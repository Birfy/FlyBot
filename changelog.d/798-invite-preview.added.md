Pasting or scanning a room invite now shows the room before anyone joins it: the join dialog asks the host for the preview and draws the room's name, its lead, how many people are in it, and how long the link lasts, with **Join** and **Clear** on that card. The name is cleaned before it is shown; a preview that cannot be fetched (a 404 or a timeout) drops the card and joins the way it did before, and an older Bot Social says invites need a newer server instead of trying a route it does not serve ([#798](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/798)).
