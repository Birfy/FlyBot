Joining a room with a link now asks the host once for every Bot picked in the join dialog; before, only the first Bot was put in the room and the others were silently left out. A Bot that cannot join is listed by name while the ones that did still bring the room up. The link asks once for the whole ticket when nothing can change for the next Bot (an unknown, expired or withdrawn invite, a room that has ended, a room that is full) and once per Bot when the refusal is only about that Bot (already in the room, its token revoked) ([#817](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/817)).
