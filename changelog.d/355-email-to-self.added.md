FlyBot can email you, at your own address, when one of your Bots finishes, fails or waits for your approval: a short line plus a link back into FlyBot, at most one email every five minutes, with no chat or tool-call content. You approve only in FlyBot; replies to the email are not read. Set your own SMTP account with `FLYBOT_SMTP_URL`, `FLYBOT_EMAIL_TO` and `FLYBOT_URL` (see the [Docker guide](docs/docker.md)), then switch on Settings → General → **Email me**, which is off by default ([#355](https://github.com/ThinkFlowLab/FlyBot/issues/355)).
